# Prompt Library

Reusable angle packs. Each = a one-line brief you drop into the Master Prompt's
`Idea` field, plus the real feature to show and hooks that tend to land. All
claims here are provable from the app.

---

## 1. Electrician pain points
**Why it works:** the trade recognizes the pain instantly → follow/comment.
**Show:** the tool that removes the pain (Conduit Fill, Voltage Drop, Sparky).
**Idea seeds:**
- "Still doing conduit fill in your head on the ladder?"
- "That feeling when the inspector asks about box fill and you're not sure."
- "Derating for 6 current-carrying conductors — from memory? Or in 5 seconds?"
**Hook patterns:** "POV: the inspector walks up and…", "Nobody talks about how…", "Stop guessing on…"

## 4. Sparky AI demos

**Show:** the app on a phone, clean, fast.

**Guardrail:** only say "live / available" if iOS status is confirmed live. Otherwise use waitlist/TestFlight CTA.

---

### Engagement levers (use across themes)
- **Ask a question** in the caption (drives comments → reach).
- **"Comment your answer"** on code questions.
- **Show the real screen** — demos convert better than talking-head for tools.
- **One feature per video** — don't cram the toolbox into 30 seconds.
