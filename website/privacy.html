Privacy

Last updated 7 August 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

There is no account. Almost everything stays on your phone. The only thing that leaves is a question you type into SparkAI — because answering it needs a server — and we do not know who you are when you send it.

What stays on your device

Your projects, job photos, daily logs, panel schedules, material lists, calculations, calculator history, flashcard and quiz progress, job-site progress, saved settings, theme and Home layout are all stored locally on your phone. None of it is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent, and when

SparkConnect makes network requests in exactly two situations:

  • You ask SparkAI a question. We send the question text, the last few messages of that conversation so the answer makes sense in context, a randomly generated device identifier, the app version, and whether the account is on the free or paid plan. If you attached a photo, the photo is sent with it.
  • You use voice input. The audio is sent to be transcribed into text, and the text is then treated as a typed question.

That is the complete list. There is no background telemetry, no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics library, and no database client in the app.

The device identifier

It is a random string generated on your phone the first time you open the app — for example dev_k3n1p8x2mfq. It is not your advertising ID, not your device serial, and not derived from anything about your hardware. It exists so daily question limits can be counted. Deleting and reinstalling the app generates a new one.

Photos

Job photos live on your phone. A photo is only ever transmitted if you attach it to a SparkAI question yourself. We do not scan your camera roll, and the app only requests photo access at the moment you use a feature that needs it.

Payments

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or store card details. Purchase status is managed through RevenueCat, which tells the app whether a plan is active. Refunds and cancellations are handled in your App Store or Google Play account.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number — the app never asks for them
  • Your location
  • Your contacts
  • Customer names or addresses you type into a project — those stay on the device and are deliberately stripped from anything sent to SparkAI

Children

SparkConnect is a professional tool intended for people working or training in the electrical trade. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off the daily notification in Settings at any time
  • Delete any project, photo or log from inside the app
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on the device
  • Email support@sparkconnect.pro with any question about your data

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the change will be described here with a new date at the top rather than quietly replacing the text.
