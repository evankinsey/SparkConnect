Troubleshooting becomes a real service call, conductors tell you what a meter would read, and the Permit Assistant gains the corrections that actually get written up.

1.3.0

Troubleshooting you have to work for

The complaint comes first and you earn the rest — walk in, meter it, kill it and ring it out, then open the boxes. Answer while four faults still fit and it tells you that you guessed. Ringing out a live circuit is refused, with the reason.

1.3.0

Tap a conductor, see what it reads

Solved per switch position by the same engine that grades the lesson. A switched leg with the switch off is pulled low through the lamp, not floating — and an open conductor is never described as reading zero.

1.3.0

Permit Assistant: what usually fails

Fourteen corrections that actually get written up, each one something you can check yourself with a tape measure before anybody schedules anything. Turnaround stays a question with somewhere to record the answer.

1.3.0

A refusal you can argue with

SparkAI now says it will not guess rather than that it cannot verify, and every refusal carries somewhere to go — plus a way to report it if it should have known.

1.3.0

Panel Schedule

Catches a multiwire branch circuit with both hots on the same phase — the neutral carries the sum of both loads and no breaker ever trips.

Projects

Verified against the printed code
